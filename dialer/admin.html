Lists

Imported contacts are not dialable until validated — the calling-hours gate needs each number's own time zone, which only Number Lookup can resolve. Validation also drops disconnected numbers before you spend a dial on them, and ranks mobiles ahead of landlines.

Validate in batches, not whole lists. At $0.0015 a number, a 100,000-contact import costs $150 to validate up front — mostly on numbers that will not be dialled for a year. Validating 500 at a time costs 75¢ and keeps agents supplied; top it up as the Unvalidated count falls.

Call recordings

Every answered call on a recording-enabled campaign, newest first. Playback links are minted on demand — Telnyx signs them for ten minutes at a time, so the URL stored against the call is dead within the hour and nothing here uses it. Pressing play asks the carrier for a fresh one, which is why there is a short pause before audio starts.

Recordings are a member of the public's own voice. Owner and admin can play any of them; everyone else only their own calls, enforced in dialer-recording rather than by hiding the button.
